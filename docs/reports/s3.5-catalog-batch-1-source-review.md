# S3.5 catalog batch 1 — nutrition-source review

## Decision

Batch 1 adds one profile: `糯米饭` (plain cooked glutinous rice). It is the highest-frequency unsupported identity in the frozen **discovery** split at 3 mentions. No holdout identity was used for selection.

Keeping the batch to one entry is intentional: it closes the highest measured catalog gap, exercises the first real substring-collision regression (`米饭 ⊂ 糯米饭`), and keeps nutrition authority independently reviewable.

## Included source and values

Authoritative source:

- Publisher: Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology of Japan (MEXT)
- Dataset: Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan, 8th revised edition, 2023 supplement
- Food item: 01154 — well-milled glutinous rice, cooked (`精白米/もち米`, cooked rice)
- Stable source: <https://fooddb.mext.go.jp/details/details.pl?ITEM_NO=1_01154_7>
- Basis: edible portion, per 100 g

| Field | MealNote value | Source value | Transformation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Energy | 188 kcal | 188 kcal | none |
| Protein | 3.5 g | 3.5 g | none |
| Fat | 0.5 g | 0.5 g | none |
| Carbohydrate | 43.9 g | 43.9 g | none |

The source describes cooked glutinous rice, not raw glutinous rice, rice flour, rice cake, sweetened sticky-rice dishes, or mixed savory glutinous-rice recipes. Those remain different foods and receive no authority from this profile.

## Identity and portion boundary

- Canonical name: `糯米饭`.
- Aliases: none. `江米饭` and other regional/product names were not present in the discovery evidence and are not added without separate review.
- Kind: `food`; plain cooked rice does not introduce an oil confirmation rule.
- Bowl/default gram assumptions reuse MealNote's existing plain-rice V1 heuristic solely for portion estimation. They are not supplied by MEXT and are not nutrition truth.
- The uncertainty range remains a MealNote product estimate for cooking-water variation; it does not alter the source's per-100-g central values.
